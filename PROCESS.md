# Process

## Tools

I used Python scripts to fetch and plot the data, and an AI assistant helped me work with the data and code. The longer data range I started with covered 1884–2026, which was more than this one-year assignment needed.

## Kept

I kept the 2025 daily NASA POWER values for Hong Kong, including both all-sky and clear-sky radiation. These two series are useful because the chart can use one for bar length and compare the other to show relative sky conditions.

## Rejected

I rejected the extra years from 1884–2024 and 2026 because they distract from the assignment's focus on one calendar year. Keeping only 2025 makes the daily pattern easier to compare around the chart and keeps the committed data relevant to the picture.
